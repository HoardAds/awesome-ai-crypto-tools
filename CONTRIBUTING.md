# Contributing

Thanks for helping keep this list useful. HoardAds maintains this list by hand — every entry is checked by a human before it goes in, and the list is updated daily.

## Two ways to contribute

1. **Suggest a tool (easiest):** open a [tool suggestion](https://github.com/HoardAds/awesome-ai-crypto-tools/issues/new/choose) and fill in the form — tool name, official website, category, a one-line description, and whether it is free.
2. **Open a pull request:** add the entry yourself in `README.md`, following the format below. One tool per pull request, please.

You can also use either route to report a dead link, a wrong description, or a tool that has shut down.

## Entry format

Each entry is a single line:

```markdown
- [Tool Name](https://official-website.example) - One sentence on what it does and who it is for.
```

Rules:

- Link to the tool's **official website** only. No affiliate links, no tracking parameters, no URL shorteners, no referral codes — they will be removed.
- One sentence: capital first letter, ends with a period, no marketing hype ("revolutionary", "best-in-class", "#1").
- Put the entry in the right category: AI Coding, AI Video, AI Writing, AI Meeting, AI Agents, Crypto, or VPN & Privacy. If none fits, say so in your suggestion and we'll consider a new category.
- Check the list first — duplicates are closed.
- The tool must be live and usable today. Waitlists, teaser pages and abandoned projects don't qualify.

## What we don't accept

- Spam, self-promotion dumps, or bulk submissions of unrelated tools.
- Phishing or look-alike sites impersonating real tools.
- Anything that asks users for private keys, seed phrases, passwords, or wallet recovery data — these are rejected on sight and reported.
- Tools whose main purpose is gambling, fraud, or evading the law.
- Dead links or sites that no longer offer the tool described.

## Review process

A maintainer checks every suggestion: the site is real, the description is accurate, and the link is clean. Approved suggestions are added in the next daily update. If a suggestion is declined, we'll say why in the issue.

Questions? Open an issue, or reach HoardAds via https://hoardads.com/contact
